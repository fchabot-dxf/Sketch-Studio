## Architecture Overview

Three apps mount their Design canvas through the shared `mountSketch(svgEl, opts)` entry point (`packages/ui/sketch-canvas.js:75`): **Shaper**, **Pen Plotter**, and **Trace**. **SketchStudio** still mounts its canvas directly via `apps/sketchstudio/main.js:78-239` and `apps/sketchstudio/design-canvas.js:14-62` (`setupInput`, `createAppHeader`, `studioFrame`). All four hosts mount `createToolRibbon` (`packages/ui/tool-ribbon.js:182`), `mountProjectSidebar` (`packages/ui/sidebar-projects.js:95`), `mountDesignBrowser` (`packages/ui/design-browser.js:90`), and `mountAppFooter` (`packages/ui/app-footer.js:44`).
