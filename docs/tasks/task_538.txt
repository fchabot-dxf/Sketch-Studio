its spring to the FIRST free joint that carries a dragTarget. So a body drag is really "drag the shape's first free joint":
a line stretches, a circle translates, an arc with a held centre does not change radius.
WANTED, ONE mechanism for every shape: the spring pulls THE POINT OF THE SHAPE THAT WAS GRABBED toward the cursor.
 - line: the grabbed point is a fixed fraction t along it: P = (1-t) A + t B. Residual P - cursor; its Jacobian spreads over A
   and B by (1-t, t). A free line grabbed in the middle then translates (both ends take the same step), grabbed near an end it
   turns about the far part: check against the Fusion cells.
 - circle: the grabbed point is on the rim at the grab angle: P = C + r * dir(theta). With the radius an unknown (515) a free
